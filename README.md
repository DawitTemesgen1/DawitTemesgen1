## 02 · Akilesiya — አቅሌስያ
**Sunday School management platform · Android · Production**

**Akilesiya is a complete digital management system for Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church Sunday Schools, designed to connect students, families, teachers, departments, and leadership in one secure platform.**

**The project was developed in collaboration with Jimma Debre Efrata Cathedral Amde Haymanot Sunday School**, helping ensure the system reflects real Sunday School workflows, responsibilities, and management needs.

Students can create accounts within their Sunday School and access their own attendance history, grades, assigned books, learning materials, and school announcements. Families can also create accounts and be securely connected to their children, allowing them to follow their child’s attendance—including late arrival times—academic performance, reading history, assigned books, and learning progress.

Sunday School departments use the platform for their actual daily responsibilities. **Mezmur, Timhirt, and Abalat Guday departments** can record attendance by day and session, mark students as present, absent, permitted, or late, and record the exact arrival time of late students.

The **Timhirt department** can create courses, record student results, define passing requirements, manage grades, and publish articles, videos, and other learning materials. The system can also show whether students accessed those materials, whether they interacted with them, and how long they spent using them.

The **Betemetsahift department** can assign books to students with deadlines and monitor their reading progress.

A particularly important part of Akilesiya is its **student development tracking system**. The Abalat Guday department can privately follow areas such as a student's behavior, habits, skills, knowledge, and overall development. This information is intentionally protected from the student and from other departments. Even the highest administrator of that Sunday School cannot access these records unless they are working through the specifically authorized Abalat Guday responsibility.

Akilesiya also includes a structured leadership system. A **System Administrator** manages the overall platform and is the only role that can oversee all Sunday Schools, create, suspend, or remove schools, and appoint each school's highest administrator.

Each Sunday School then has its own **Superior Administrator**, who manages that school, verifies newly registered users, connects family accounts to students, and assigns responsibilities such as teachers, section administrators, family management, and other leadership roles. A newly created account does not automatically receive access to Sunday School information until it has been verified.

The system also helps departments manage their **annual plans**. Departments can define activities, schedule them by month, assign responsible people, track progress, and mark work as completed. At the end of the Ethiopian year, the year's plan can be officially closed and preserved as historical data while a new planning year begins.

For accountability, the Superior Administrator has access to a detailed **audit history** showing important changes across the Sunday School, including attendance, grades, plans, user activity, and departmental activity, with both the previous and updated information recorded.

The platform can also generate and print structured student records directly from the application.

I worked on Akilesiya as a **Full-Stack and Mobile App Developer**, developing the mobile application, backend system, database, access-control structure, departmental workflows, family and student systems, attendance and academic management, auditing, reporting, and production deployment.

I also handled the application's **Android production release and Google Play deployment**.

## 03 · አርያም
**Offline liturgical application · Android · In testing**

**አርያም is an Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church liturgical application that makes daily and weekly Mahlet, Misbak, Zimare, Militan, Gitsawe, and related readings easier to access in one place.**

The project was built in collaboration with the **Yaredawuyan Telegram channel**, which regularly publishes daily and weekly Mahlet, Misbak, Zimare, Militan, and related liturgical content. The application helps organize that knowledge into a more structured, searchable, and reusable digital system.

Instead of requiring users to manually search through different sources, አርያም helps them find the appropriate **Mahlet, Misbak, Zimare, Militan, Gitsawe, and New Testament readings for a specific day without requiring an internet connection**.

A major part of the application is its use of **Bahre Hasab** and liturgical rules to determine date-based content correctly. It can calculate movable feasts, identify Sundays and important dates, and select the appropriate liturgical content based on the day and season.

The application is currently **under testing and final preparation** and is planned for release on the **Google Play Store**.

## 04 · Amde Haymanot Official Website
**Multilingual publishing platform · Web · Production**

**Amde Haymanot Official Website is the official digital platform of Jimma Debre Efrata Cathedral Amde Haymanot Sunday School, built to bring the Sunday School’s teaching, announcements, events, media, and public information into one organized online platform.**

The website gives members and visitors a central place to follow the Sunday School, access educational content, view events and announcements, explore galleries and media, and stay connected with its activities in **eight national and international languages**, making the platform accessible to a much wider audience.

The platform also includes an administration system that allows authorized users to manage website content, courses, events, galleries, media, announcements, submissions, and other public information without directly editing the website code.

One of the most important parts of the platform is its **AI-assisted Telegram publishing system**. When the Sunday School publishes a post on its official Telegram channel, an integrated bot detects the new content, processes it, identifies whether it should be published as news or an event, and prepares it for the website. The system then translates the content into the **eight languages supported by the website** and publishes the structured multilingual version to the Sunday School website, significantly reducing repetitive manual publishing work.

The platform also supports large media collections, including optimized image handling, audio processing, gallery management, and tools for preparing and publishing content efficiently.

## 05 · Amde Haymanot Zimare
**Digital mezmur platform · Android · 15K+ downloads**

**Amde Haymanot Zimare is a digital Ethiopian Orthodox mezmur platform built to preserve, organize, publish, and make mezmur lyrics (gitim) and audio easily accessible both online and without an internet connection.**

The project was developed in collaboration with **Jimma Debre Efrata Cathedral Amde Haymanot Sunday School**.

Users can browse and search mezmur, read lyrics (gitim), listen to audio, save favorites, and download mezmur audio for offline listening. The application also allows users to check for newly published mezmur and automatically update its content when new lyrics (gitim) become available, storing the updated content locally so it remains accessible without internet access.

The platform includes a management system where authorized administrators can **add, edit, remove, and manage mezmur lyrics (gitim) and audio**. This makes the application’s content continuously maintainable without requiring a new version of the mobile app whenever mezmur content changes.

When a mezmur audio file is submitted, the system automatically processes it into **two downloadable versions**: a smaller, data-friendly version for faster downloads and lower storage usage, and a higher-quality version with a larger file size. Both versions are available in the application, and users can choose their preferred download quality from the app settings.

A major part of the project is its **AI-assisted Telegram contribution system**. Members of the Mezmur department—or anyone who has mezmur content to contribute—can communicate directly with a dedicated Telegram bot. The AI-integrated bot interacts with contributors in a respectful and conversational way, accepts mezmur lyrics (gitim) and audio, organizes the submission, and sends it to the application's administration portal.

Administrators can then review the submitted mezmur, make necessary corrections, and approve or reject it. Once approved, the content is added to the central database and becomes available to application users. This creates a structured workflow for turning community-contributed mezmur into reviewed and published digital content.

Together, the mobile application, administration portal, and Telegram bot provide a complete system for **collecting, reviewing, preserving, processing, distributing, and continuously updating Ethiopian Orthodox mezmur content**.

I worked on the project as a **Flutter Mobile App and Full-Stack Developer**, developing the mobile application, content synchronization and offline system, audio playback and downloads, audio processing workflow, administration tools, backend integration, and the AI-powered Telegram contribution workflow.
